# Project 5 Interview Discussion Guide

## Your 60-Second Explanation

> I built a SOC 2 readiness and evidence-management package for Northbridge Cloudworks. It includes a control matrix, PBC evidence tracker, findings register, remediation tracker, and readiness dashboard. The goal was to show how a GRC analyst helps a company prepare before an audit by confirming control owners, evidence status, operating effectiveness, exceptions, remediation, and executive readiness reporting.

## Where You Sound Different From Other Candidates

Say this:

> SOC 2 readiness is not just collecting screenshots. I would verify whether evidence supports the control objective, covers the right population and period, shows approval where required, and documents exceptions and remediation.

That is a highly competent answer because it shows you understand audit evidence quality.

## Likely Interview Questions and Strong Answer Frames

### Question: What is a PBC list?

Answer:

> PBC stands for prepared by client. It is the evidence request list that the company provides to auditors. A GRC analyst helps track the request, owner, due date, status, evidence link, auditor comments, and follow-up actions.

### Question: How would you prepare a company for SOC 2?

Answer:

> I would start with scope, systems, control owners, criteria mapping, evidence requirements, and readiness testing. Then I would identify missing evidence, control exceptions, remediation owners, and whether the company is ready for an audit period.

### Question: What makes evidence good or bad?

Answer:

> Good evidence is complete, dated, tied to the control objective, covers the correct population and period, shows approval if required, and includes exception handling. Bad evidence is stale, incomplete, manually edited without support, missing owner approval, or not tied to the control.

### Question: What is the difference between SOC 2 readiness and a SOC 2 audit?

Answer:

> Readiness is an internal preparation exercise to find and fix gaps before the audit. The audit is performed by an independent CPA firm against the Trust Services Criteria.

### Question: What would you escalate?

Answer:

> I would escalate high-risk access-control exceptions, missing incident-response exercise evidence, overdue critical vulnerability remediation, and evidence gaps that could affect audit timing or customer commitments.

## STAR Story Framework

Situation: Northbridge wanted to understand whether it was ready for future SOC 2 work.

Task: Build a readiness package that organized controls, evidence, findings, and remediation.

Action: Created a control matrix, PBC tracker, findings register, remediation tracker, and executive dashboard.

Result: Identified readiness gaps and produced a practical 60-90 day remediation plan before formal audit engagement.

## Role-Play Scenarios

### Role-Play 1 - Auditor Evidence Request

Interviewer: "The auditor asks for MFA evidence. What do you provide?"

Your response:

> I would provide the MFA policy configuration, active user population, MFA enrollment report, privileged-user listing, exception approvals, and evidence that exceptions were reviewed and remediated. I would make sure the evidence covers the audit period and scoped systems.

### Role-Play 2 - Control Owner Pushback

Interviewer: "The control owner says they do not have time to gather evidence."

Your response:

> I would explain the business impact: missing evidence can delay audit readiness and customer assurance. I would clarify the exact request, due date, acceptable evidence, and help reduce ambiguity, but the control owner still owns the evidence.

### Role-Play 3 - Executive Briefing

Interviewer: "Brief the executive team in two minutes."

Your response:

> Northbridge has a solid foundation but is not fully SOC 2 ready yet. The main blockers are access-control evidence, incident-response testing, vulnerability SLA evidence, and centralized evidence ownership. I recommend a 60-90 day readiness sprint before starting an audit period.

## Questions You Can Ask The Interviewer

- Is your team currently preparing for SOC 2, maintaining SOC 2, or responding to customer assurance requests?
- What GRC tool do you use for evidence tracking?
- Who owns evidence collection: GRC, control owners, or both?
- What are your most common audit evidence gaps?
